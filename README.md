# Spoken Saudi Arabic for anaesthetists

102 phrases from the anaesthetic room to recovery, each spoken by a Saudi voice. Where the Arabic changes for a man or a woman, both forms are there.

Open it on your phone: https://gundoc9.github.io/spoken-saudi-arabic/

- Play any phrase, slower if you need it, or turn on Listen & repeat
- Test yourself with the Arabic hidden, or take the ten-question listening quiz
- Hear what patients often say back, from a man and from a woman

Risks and the full consent go through an interpreter.

Phrases by Dr Ganesh Sivasankara · MD · FRCA · FCARCSI · Consultant Anaesthetist.
Voices: Fahad and Layan, AI voices made with ElevenLabs.
